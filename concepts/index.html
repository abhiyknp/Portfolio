02 · Proof, not claims

What my acceptance criteria look like.

Illustrative example

As a reviewing officer, I want to raise a clarification with a specific reason, so that the applicant knows exactly what to fix.

  • Given an application assigned to me, when I raise a clarification with a reason, then the applicant is notified and the authority clock pauses.
  • Given no reason is selected, then I see "Select at least one reason" and nothing is saved.
  • Given I'm not the assigned reviewer, then the action isn't available.
  • Given the applicant responds, then the file returns to my queue marked "changed".

Case study · Government

Rajmarg Pravesh: one permission, end to end.

Read on the left. The diagram follows you, lighting up the part of the workflow each step is about.

01 · Context

A national portal for highway access

Applicants ask for permission to connect to a National Highway or use its Right of Way. Several authorities review, inspect, collect fees and approve.

From résumé and project brief
02 · Submission

Nothing reaches review incomplete

The applicant marks the location on a map, enters chainage (km) and uploads documents. Submission is blocked until every mandatory document for that application type is attached.

Illustrative rule
03 · Clarification

Ask once, precisely, and pause the clock

If something is wrong, the reviewer raises a clarification with a reason. The authority's turnaround clock pauses while the applicant fixes it, then resumes.

Illustrative example
04 · Inspection and approval

Site checked, then provisional approval

A field inspection report goes back to the reviewer, who recommends. The approving authority grants provisional approval, returns the file, or rejects it with reasons.

Illustrative example
05 · Payment and guarantee

Money confirmed, guarantees valid

Fees are paid through BharatKosh. If a bank guarantee's validity ends too early, finance returns it and the applicant re-uploads, without starting again.

06 · eSign

Final approval, digitally signed

The approver signs with eSign and the applicant downloads the permission.
